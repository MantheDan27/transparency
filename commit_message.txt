🎨 Palette: [UX improvement] Hide decorative SVGs from screen readers

💡 What: Added `aria-hidden="true"` to all 7 decorative SVG icons in the UI.
🎯 Why: Prevents screen readers from announcing meaningless image descriptions or attempting to interpret SVG elements, making navigation cleaner.
📸 Before/After: N/A (invisible accessibility change)
♿ Accessibility: Improved screen reader experience by explicitly hiding decorative SVG icons that are either paired with visible text or contained within elements that already have an `aria-label`.
